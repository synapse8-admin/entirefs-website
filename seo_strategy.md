# SEO Strategy — Entire Financial Services

## Site overview
Financial advisory firm based in Mulgrave, Victoria, Australia. Specialises in retirement planning, transition to retirement, aged care financial advice, superannuation, and related services. Operates as a corporate authorised representative of Apex Macro Financial Group.

## Stack
React SPA (Vite + Wouter). All page content is client-side rendered. There is no SSR or SSG layer.

## In scope
- Home page (`/`)
- About page (`/about`)
- Service pages: `/retirement-planning`, `/transition-to-retirement`, `/aged-care`, `/services/superannuation`
- Contact page (`/contact`)
- Ancillary pages: `/privacy-policy`, `/complaints`, `/financial-services-guide`

## Out of scope
- Authenticated pages (none present)

## Target audience
Australians approaching or in retirement (50+), individuals considering aged care for themselves or a family member, people seeking superannuation advice.

## Primary keywords / topics
- Retirement planning Melbourne / Victoria
- Transition to retirement advice
- Aged care financial planning
- Superannuation financial adviser
- Financial adviser Mulgrave

Build a polished, responsive front-end website mockup for Entire Financial Services.

This is a mockup-first build for client review, not a final production deployment. The outcome should look like a premium, modern, trustworthy financial services website with reusable components, clean structure, and realistic placeholder content where final content is not yet available.

PROJECT GOAL
Create a review-ready website mockup that presents Entire Financial Services as professional, credible, and approachable, while supporting lead generation and required disclosure content.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT
- This is for Entire Financial Services.
- The design should feel premium, calm, modern, professional, spacious, and trustworthy.

- The original brief called for a website mockup with core pages including Home, About Us, Services, Contact, and Privacy Policy, plus room for other pages and functionality.
- The site also needs Testimonials and a Financial Services Guide page.
- The website must support adviser details, disclosure content, privacy information, complaints handling, and contact details.

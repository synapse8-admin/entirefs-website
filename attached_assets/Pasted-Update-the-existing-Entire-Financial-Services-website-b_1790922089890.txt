## Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Entire Financial Services (“Entire Financial Services”, “Entire FS”, “we”, “our” or “us”) respects the privacy of the people we deal with and is committed to protecting the personal information entrusted to us.

We are bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and manage personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

We may amend this Privacy Policy from time to time where required by law, changes to our business practices or developments in technology. The latest version will be made available through our website.

## The Information We Collect

To provide financial advice and related services, we may need to collect personal information about you.

Depending on the services you require, this may include:

- your name, date of birth and contact details;
- identification information;
- employment and income information;
- details of your assets and liabilities;
- superannuation and investment information;
- insurance information;
- taxation information;
- Centrelink or other government benefit information;
- information about your financial objectives, needs and priorities;
- marital and family circumstances;
- transaction and account information; and
- other information relevant to the financial advice or services you have requested.

We may also collect copies or details of identification documents such as your driver's licence or passport where required to verify your identity.

If you do not provide information that we reasonably require, we may not be able to provide appropriate advice or services to you.

## Health Information

In some circumstances, particularly where personal insurance products are being considered or arranged, we may need to collect health information.

Health information will only be collected where it is relevant to the service being provided and where its collection is permitted by law.

## Sensitive Information

- otherwise provide services that you have requested or authorised.

Without the information reasonably required by us, we may be unable to provide you with advice or an appropriate level of service.

## Non-indexable responses and exclusions

- Unknown routes: genuine 404, noindex/nofollow, no canonical or schema, excluded from sitemap.
- Preview/staging and deployment aliases: noindex/nofollow in HTML and X-Robots-Tag; robots disallows crawling and sitemap is 404. These responses intentionally do not claim production identity. Existing indexed staging URLs may need owner-led removal/recrawl; robots blocking alone is not a removal guarantee.
- No fabricated ratings, reviews, opening hours, coordinates, licence claims or ownership tokens were added to structured data. JSON-LD parses and agrees with the supplied visible business details; live Rich Results eligibility remains a manual check.
- Main Services navigation targets /#services rather than an invented standalone route. Superannuation has an inbound Home link.
- Query strings do not appear in canonicals; www HTTPS, lowercase known routes and no trailing slash except root are the policy.
- Real site-verification tags appear only when nonempty approved tokens are provided at build time. No Google/Bing verification has occurred.
